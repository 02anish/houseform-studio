A LITTLE GUIDANCE

From a blank canvas to a home.

Build: choose a room, wall, door, window, stairs, or roof, then click the canvas. A room creates a floor surface and four editable walls.

Openings: place doors and windows near a wall. They cut real openings and follow the wall. Select a linked wall and detach it to edit independently.

Edit: select and drag items. Properties adjust exact dimensions, elevation, rotation, and material. Duplicate and delete any item.

Levels: add floors, set their height, size, and finish. Toggle All floors in 3D to see the house stacked.

Materials: click a material to apply it to the selection, or create your own finish.

Shortcuts: Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates, Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes, Escape cancels. In 3D, drag empty space to orbit, right-drag to pan, or drag an item to move it.

Projects save in this browser. Export JSON for backup or sharing. This editor creates concept designs; it does not produce construction drawings or structural calculations.
